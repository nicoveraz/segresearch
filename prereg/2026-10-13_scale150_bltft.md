# Pre-registration: novelty patching at 150M and in a fully fine-tuned BLT-1B (local, private)

**Written:** 2026-10-13, before any 150M or BLT-1B fine-tuning run, and before the 50M clipping checks and novelty
seeds 1-2 are known. **Status:** DRAFT until the two gates below are evaluated; predictions and thresholds are
fixed now and must not be edited after the gates are read (amendments go in a dated section at the end).

## Evidence so far (what motivates this)
- 12M, unclipped, 3 seeds (Kaggle T4): nov10 final answers 71.5% vs entropy10 46.6% (+24.8, seeds separate) and
  dep10 56.7% (+14.8, seeds separate); computed results 10.1% vs 5.8% / 7.7% (not separated).
- 50M, clipped, seed 0 only: nov10 final 90.6%, computed 56.3%, vs the pilot's unclipped entropy10 59.0 / 13.9,
  dep10 78.9 / 35.9, syntax+entropy10 85.5 / 43.3.
- typed14 fails when trained (12M final 18.9%, 50M 50.6%), though it helps BLT-1B at inference: not carried forward
  as a training rule here.

## Gates (evaluated on the running 50M pod job; no new spending until both pass)
- **G1, clipping neutrality.** Clipped entropy10 and dep10 (seed 0) at 50M are within the pilot's unclipped
  per-seed range, widened by 3 points, on final answers and on computed results. If not, novelty's 50M lead is
  re-stated against the clipped baselines only, and the 150M study (below) is unaffected because every 150M arm is
  trained clipped.
- **G2, 50M replication.** nov10 seeds 1 and 2 (clipped) each exceed the mean of the clipped dep10 check (or, if
  G1 passes, of the pilot's dep10) by at least 10 points on final answers AND by at least 10 points on computed
  results.
- If G2 fails, stop: write up the 12M result and the 50M seeds as they are; no 150M or fine-tuning spend.

## Study A: trained from scratch at 145M parameters
- **Model and data:** the scaling pilot's architecture and corpus (OpenWebMath shards 0-3 + GSM8K/MATH train, 1.90 GB,
  SHA-256 pinned), size "150m" in scale/prep.py: width 832, 16 global layers, 145M parameters, 91,500 updates of
  256 x 128 bytes (~3.0B bytes), lr 4.6e-4 (3e-3 x 128/832), warmup 2,000, gradient clipping 1.0 for every arm.
- **Arms:** entropy10, dep10, nov10 (thresholds fitted on the corpus, as in the pilot). **Seeds:** 0 and 1.
- **Metrics:** primary: final-answer accuracy and computed-result accuracy (greedy, boundaries decided online,
  1000 targets each, as in the pilot). Secondary: bits per byte, MATH boxed, end-to-end GSM8K, eval patch rate.
- **Predictions:**
  - **A1:** nov10 - entropy10 on final answers >= +15 points, every nov10 seed above every entropy10 seed.
  - **A2:** nov10 - dep10 on computed results > 0, every nov10 seed above every dep10 seed.
  - **A3 (trend):** the nov10 - entropy10 gap on final answers at 145M is at least the 50M gap minus 5 points
    (it does not collapse with scale).
  - **A4 (cost):** nov10's bits per byte within 0.03 of entropy10's.
- **Failure:** nov10 - entropy10 on final answers < +5 points, or below entropy10 on computed results.

## Study B: Meta's BLT-1B fully fine-tuned at a 10% patch budget
- **Model:** itazap/blt-1b-hf with the 512-byte window restored (blt_load.py); all weights trained.
- **Data:** GSM8K and MATH training solutions written as in realblt_budget.py ("The final answer is N"); the same
  data, steps and optimizer for every arm.
- **Arms (patch layouts during fine-tuning AND evaluation):** entropy@10 (control: BLT's own rule at the budget),
  nov@10, nov+final@10 (boundary after "The final answer is "). Reference without training: stock BLT-1B, default
  patching (~26% of bytes). **Seeds:** 0 and 1.
- **Metrics:** primary: end-to-end GSM8K test accuracy (greedy generation from the question, patch boundaries
  decided online, answer read after "The final answer is"). Secondary: teacher-forced computed-result and
  final-answer exact match (as in the paper), MATH-500 end to end, compute per byte (flops.py).
- **Predictions:**
  - **B1:** nov+final@10 > entropy@10 on end-to-end GSM8K by >= 5 points (pooled over seeds; paired McNemar
    p < 0.05).
  - **B2:** nov+final@10 at 10% reaches at least 90% of the stock model's end-to-end GSM8K accuracy at ~26%
    (if the stock model's accuracy is above 5%; otherwise B2 is not evaluated).
  - **B3:** nov@10 without the cue is below nov+final@10 on final answers (as at inference).
- **Failure:** nov+final@10 - entropy@10 <= 0 on end-to-end GSM8K.

## Amendments
- **2026-10-13, A-1 (before G2 is complete and before any Study A or B spending).** Motivation: at 12M (Kaggle,
  3 seeds, unclipped) the novelty table fitted on the training corpus itself (novc13: novelty_corpus.npz from 4M
  bytes of the 1.9 GB corpus; 13% of corpus bytes = 11.4% of eval bytes) reached final answers 69.8% vs the
  math-fitted nov10's 71.5%, with the best bits per byte at 12M (1.619). It is the more general rule (no curated
  math text), so Study A tests both novelty tables:
  - **Study A arms:** entropy10, dep10, nov10, novc13; seeds 0 and 1 (8 runs).
  - **Predictions A1-A4 apply to nov10 and to novc13 separately**, each against the same entropy10 and dep10 runs.
  - **New prediction A5:** novc13 is within 5 points of nov10 on final answers at 145M (mean over seeds).
  - **Study B unchanged:** its novelty table (realblt_combo.py, fitted on GSM8K training solutions) is already
    fitted on the fine-tuning text itself, the analogue of novc for that setting.
  - Cost estimate for Study A rises from 6 to 8 runs (~$200-330 on rented H100s).
- **2026-10-13, A-2 (after G2 failed): Studies A and B run as EXPLORATORY, not confirmatory.** Decision by the PI
  after the G2 miss. Disclosed with it: the G2 threshold (every seed >= 10 points over dep10 on final answers) was a
  round number, not derived from the measurement's uncertainty. Measured afterwards: one run's final-answer score
  has a binomial sampling error of ~1.2 points (660 targets; 95% +-2.3) and seeds spread ~1 point, so the gap of
  means is +10.7 [+8.3, +13.1] (computed results +21.6 [+15.9, +27.2]); a per-seed >= 10 rule on a ~10.7-point
  effect passes or fails largely by chance. Predictions A1-A5 and B1-B3 are kept as written and will be reported
  against, but as exploratory evidence. Future gates use the lower bound of a 95% interval against a margin, set
  from the measurement's known uncertainty.
- **2026-10-13, A-3 (supersedes A-2's "exploratory" label; written before ANY Study A or B data exists).**
  - **Status.** G2 is a spending gate, not a hypothesis of Studies A or B. It stays recorded as NOT MET as written
    (seed 2: +9.7 vs the threshold 10). The PI proceeds anyway; this is disclosed with the reason: the threshold was
    a round number at the effect size, not derived from the measurement's uncertainty. Studies A and B remain
    confirmatory tests of THEIR OWN predictions, because those are fixed here before their data.
  - **Post hoc, for the record only (not used for any claim):** under the decision rule below, G2's final-answer
    gap (+10.7 [+8.3, +13.1]) exceeds a +5 margin and computed results (+21.6 [+15.9, +27.2]) exceed +10.
  - **Known measurement uncertainty (50M, measured):** one run's final-answer score has a binomial SE of ~1.2
    points (660 targets), computed results ~1.6 (1000 targets); seed-to-seed SD ~1 point (final) and 1-3 points
    (computed). This sets the margins below.
  - **Decision rule (replaces every per-seed threshold in A1-A5 and B1-B3).** For each prediction, a 95% interval
    for the stated difference: Welch over seeds for Study A (scale/compare_new.py); a paired bootstrap over test
    problems within seeds, pooled over seeds, for Study B. HOLDS if the whole interval is beyond the margin;
    CONTRADICTED if the whole interval is on the wrong side of it; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. All reported.
  - **Study A (145M): 3 seeds per rule** (entropy10, dep10, nov10, novc13; 12 runs), so the Welch interval is not
    dominated by the t-quantile of two seeds.
    - A1: nov10 - entropy10, final answers, margin +10 points (50M gap: +30.5).
    - A2: nov10 - dep10, computed results, margin 0.
    - A3: [nov10 - entropy10 at 145M] - [the same at 50M], final answers, margin -5 (the gap does not shrink by more).
    - A4: nov10 - entropy10, bits per byte, upper margin +0.03.
    - A5: novc13 - nov10, final answers: HOLDS if the interval lies within [-5, +5] (equivalence).
    - A1, A2 and A4 are evaluated for novc13 as well.
  - **Study B (BLT-1B fine-tune): 2 seeds per arm, 500 end-to-end GSM8K problems** (was 300) to narrow the paired
    interval; 300 teacher-forced problems as before.
    - B1: nov+final@10 - entropy@10, end-to-end GSM8K, margin 0 (and the point estimate reported against +5).
    - B2: nov+final@10 / stock BLT-1B (default patching) end to end, margin 0.90 on the ratio (evaluated only if the
      stock model scores above 5%).
    - B3: nov+final@10 - nov@10, teacher-forced final answers, margin 0.
  - **Cost** with 3 seeds in Study A: ~$300-500 on rented H100s; Study B ~$70-170.
